`wayfinder:grilling` · HITL · blocked by: nothing · assignee: —

## Question

The everyday jobs, in order of how often they happen. Not what the app can do —
what Shaf opens it to do.

Known from watching it this week: *find out who is stuck and answer them* happens
constantly (seven agents sat on one question for hours). *Start a new agent in a
folder* happens daily. *Read what an agent is saying* happens often. Everything
else — connecting agents, running a workflow, planning a team — has happened zero
times on this machine, on the evidence of the stores on disk.

So the question is: which of those zeros are **"I never wanted it"** and which are
**"it never worked well enough to try"**? Those two answers lead to opposite
products.

**Why it blocks:** ranks everything. *The one connection model*, *the queue* and
*how many places the nav goes* all depend on which jobs are real.
