## Question

Three candidates, and the decision is which is the default — `config.json` should
carry all three, so this is about what opens, not what is possible.

- **By Herdr window** — today. `logistics-T4` is five boxes named after windows you
  opened by accident.
- **By folder** — one heading per project, three headings for twelve agents.
  Nothing new is invented; the folder is already on every card.
- **By state** — Agent Orchestrator's kanban. Needs *Next up* to have a queue and
  *In review* to have facts, or two of five columns are permanently empty.

Shaf has said he likes the window boxes' look. So this is not "delete them" — it is
whether the same visual treatment can hold a folder or a state instead of a window
name, and whether twelve agents then fit one screen.

Resolve by building all three as a rough page against the live snapshot and looking
at them side by side, not by argument.
